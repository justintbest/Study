 Overview
 Check this page periodically for future updates.
 By the time candidates sit for the California Supplemental Examination (CSE), they have already met a rigorous set of general educational and professional
 requirements. The CSE tests a representative sample of the knowledge necessary to perform the architect profession in California in a safe and competent 
 manner, not whether candidates have an encyclopedic knowledge of California's statutes and regulations, therefore a comprehensive list of reference 
 materials is inconsistent with the purpose of the CSE. The list below is intended to help candidates prepare to take the CSE and not to cover every single 
 knowledge addressed in the examination.
