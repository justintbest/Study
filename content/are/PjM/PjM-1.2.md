## What this objective tests
As the project runs, are the right people on it, and is it on track for **hours, fee and schedule**? It's A/E: interpret project data, such as hours spent, percent complete and burn rate, and decide what to do.

## The core measurements
| Measure | Formula | What it tells you |
|---|---|---|
| Fee earned | % complete × phase fee | Value of the work actually done |
| Fee spent | Labor cost charged so far | What it cost to get here |
| Variance today | Earned − spent | Over or under budget now |
| Projected cost at completion | Spent ÷ % complete | Where you'll end up at the current pace |
| Cost performance index (CPI) | Earned ÷ spent | Above 1 = under budget; below 1 = over |
| Burn rate | Hours per week × rates | Weekly spending, so you can forecast when the fee runs out |
| Hours available | Fee ÷ blended billing rate | Size of the staffing plan |

> [!TRAP] "We've spent 75% of the fee, so we're 75% done." **Percent spent is not percent complete.** Estimate completion from the work remaining, task by task.

## Staffing by phase
- **Schematic design:** a small, senior-heavy team.
- **Design development:** the team grows, and consultants are fully engaged.
- **Construction documents:** the **peak** in staff and hours.
- **Construction administration:** a smaller, specialized team (submittals, RFIs, site visits). Senior design input continues where design intent is at stake.

## When the numbers go wrong
1. **Diagnose** task by task: where did the hours go?
2. **Separate scope growth from inefficiency.** Out-of-scope work becomes an additional-service request with authorization (see 4.2 and PcM 2.3).
3. **Re-plan the remaining work.** Shift suitable tasks to lower-rate staff with supervision, and remove rework.
4. **Communicate:** principal first, then the client if the schedule or scope is affected.
5. **Don't cut QC reviews** to save hours. Errors cost more later.

## Managing people across projects
- Watch for overloaded key staff (utilization above 100% across projects). Rebalance and coordinate priorities with leadership. Use overtime only briefly.
- Adding many people late to a struggling phase can slow it down, because of onboarding and coordination. Add people with a revised plan.

## Common exam traps
> [!TRAP] Treating today's variance as the final overrun. Project to completion using the current performance.

> [!TRAP] Solving an overrun by skipping QC or quietly doing less than the scope requires.

> [!TRAP] Billing extra work without prior written authorization.
