## What this objective tests
Can you plan and manage communication within the project team (owner, architect, consultants, contractor) so that decisions are made, recorded and sent to the right people? It's U/A. The tools are named in the objective: **agendas, meeting minutes, emails and memos.**

## Communication plan
At the start, agree with the owner on:
- **who talks to whom:** each party's single point of contact, and who can make decisions;
- **channels:** meetings, email, the project portal, transmittals;
- **frequency:** regular meetings and reports;
- **distribution:** who gets what (minutes, ASIs, change orders, schedules).

## Agendas and meetings
- Send the agenda **in advance**, listing the **decisions needed** and any background material, so attendees arrive ready to decide.
- Invite the people who have the information and the authority.
- Close each item with a decision or an action item.

## Minutes
- Record **decisions, action items (who and when) and open issues**, not a transcript.
- Send them promptly to all attendees and the agreed distribution list.
- State that corrections are due within a set number of days. Otherwise the minutes stand as accurate.
- When someone asks for a correction, **record it in the next minutes**, which go to everyone, rather than in a private reply.

## Emails, memos and transmittals
- **Email:** quick coordination. Keep project email organized and filed by project.
- **Memo or letter:** decisions, impacts on cost, schedule or scope, recommendations and anything needing a formal record. Follow it with a discussion where useful.
- **Transmittal:** records what was sent, to whom and when (e.g., the 95% CD set). It prevents "we never got it" disputes.
- **Verbal direction:** confirm it in writing promptly, then process it through the proper document: an ASI, a CCD or a change order.
- **Decision log:** collects owner decisions in one list. Get the owner's written approval at the end of each phase.

## Formal channels during construction
A201-2017 routes communication:
- the owner and contractor include the architect in communications that affect the architect's services;
- consultant communications go **through the architect**;
- subcontractor communications go **through the contractor**;
- separate-contractor communications go **through the owner**.

> [!VERIFY] A201-2017 §4.2.4 (communications) is from my knowledge of the 2017 edition. Confirm in your copy.

**During bidding:** answer a bidder's question about the documents by **addendum to all bidders**, never in a private conversation.

## Common exam traps
> [!TRAP] Letting a subcontractor work directly with the architect's consultant. Questions go through the contractor (as an RFI) and then through the architect.

> [!TRAP] Acting on an owner's verbal site instruction without confirming it in writing.

> [!TRAP] Minutes that record discussion but not who will do what by when.
