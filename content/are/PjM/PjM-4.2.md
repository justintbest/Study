## What this objective tests
When the scope changes, can you evaluate what it does to the **architect's fee and staffing, the project schedule, and the owner's budget and financing**, and handle the change under the agreement? It's A/E. Owner-requested additions, deletions and late changes are the usual scenarios.

## Evaluate every change on four fronts
| Impact | Questions |
|---|---|
| **Construction cost** | What does it add or delete? Contingency and escalation? |
| **Architect's fee** | Redesign of finished work, plus more effort in the remaining phases, plus consultants × markup |
| **Schedule** | Does it hit the critical path? Permit resubmittal? Owner approvals? Lead times? |
| **Owner's financing** | Loan limits, equity, grant or rate-lock deadlines, carrying costs of delay |

## Handling it under B101-2017
1. **Recognize it.** A change in the owner's instructions or approvals, or a material change in size, quality, complexity, schedule or budget, is a §4.2.2 additional service.
2. **Notify the owner promptly in writing.** Explain the need, the fee, and the schedule effect.
3. **Get written authorization before proceeding.** During construction, §4.2.3 services, such as evaluating change-order proposals, are provided to avoid delay, with prompt notice.
4. **Adjust the schedule** with the owner, and update the work plan and budget (see 1.2, 2.1).
5. **Track small changes as they accumulate.** Many "small favors" add up to real overruns. Bundle them into regular additional-service requests **before** doing the work.

> [!VERIFY] §4.2.2 and §4.2.3 categories are from my knowledge of B101-2017.

## Fee calculations
- **Added area or scope mid-design:**
  - fee for the phases still to come × the percentage increase in scope;
  - plus hours to redesign completed work;
  - plus consultant fees × markup.
- **Hourly not-to-exceed (NTE):** bill the actual hours × rates, up to the cap. The cap is a ceiling, not the amount to bill.
- **Deleted scope on a stipulated sum:** an equitable adjustment that accounts for work already done on the deleted portion. Not a straight proportional cut.

## Schedule and financing calculations
- **Delay cost** = escalation over the delay (cost × annual rate × weeks ÷ 52) + the owner's carrying costs (rent, interest, lost revenue) for the delay.
- **Loan-limited financing:** if the loan is capped, cost added above the cap is **all owner equity**.

## Common exam traps
> [!TRAP] Treating the change as only a construction-cost question. The exam expects fee, schedule and financing too.

> [!TRAP] Billing the full NTE amount instead of the actual hours.

> [!TRAP] Doing the redesign first and asking for authorization afterward.
