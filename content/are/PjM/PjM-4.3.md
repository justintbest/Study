## What this objective tests
Different delivery methods need different documents at different times. Can you choose and manage the right documentation? That means a complete bid set, GMP and early-release packages, bridging and performance documents, conformed and record sets, and properly tracked revisions. It's A/E.

## Documentation by delivery method
| Method | What the architect produces |
|---|---|
| **Design-bid-build** | Complete CDs and a project manual that can be priced as a fixed sum. Procurement and contracting requirements (invitation, instructions to bidders, bid form). **Allowances and alternates** for decisions that aren't final |
| **CM as constructor (CMc)** | Documents at the stage the GMP is set (often late DD or partial CDs), reconciled against the CM's **assumptions and clarifications** list. **Early-release packages** (site, foundations, steel). The remaining documents complete the design |
| **Design-build (owner's bridging architect)** | **Bridging documents**: program, design concept, site and performance criteria, carried only far enough to define what the owner requires (often about 30% design). **Performance specifications** leave the detailed design to the design-builder |
| **Design-build (architect on the design-builder's team)** | Full documents for the design-builder. The client is the design-builder, under its agreement |
| **Multiple prime / fast-track packages** | Clear **package boundaries**: a written scope for each package, drawings showing limits, and a list of shared or interface items |
| **Integrated project delivery** | Shared models developed to the agreed **level of development (LOD)** at each milestone, under a BIM execution plan |

## Documents during and after bidding
- **Addenda:** changes during bidding, issued to all bidders.
- **Conformed documents:** the bid set with the addenda folded in, for construction. A supplemental service in B101-2017.
- **ASIs, bulletins, CCDs and change orders:** changes during construction. Identify each revision with clouds and deltas, update the title-block issue log, and list the revised sheets on a transmittal.
- **Record documents:**
  - the contractor keeps marked-up as-built drawings at the site;
  - the architect may prepare record drawings from them, as a supplemental service;
  - record drawings reflect what the contractor reported, not what the architect verified.

## Specification approaches
| Type | Use |
|---|---|
| Prescriptive / descriptive | When the design is set: materials and methods described |
| Proprietary | When a specific product is needed |
| Performance | Design-build, delegated design: required results and criteria |
| Reference standard | Refers to ASTM or industry standards |

**Delegated design** (A201-2017 §3.12.10): the documents state the **performance and design criteria**. The contractor's licensed professional designs the item and seals it. The architect reviews it for conformance with the design concept.

> [!VERIFY] A201-2017 §3.12.10 and the B101 supplemental-service list are from my knowledge of the 2017 editions.

## Common exam traps
> [!TRAP] Over-detailing bridging documents. That takes on design responsibility and removes the design-builder's flexibility.

> [!TRAP] Leaving an undecided owner item out of a DBB bid set. Use an **allowance** instead.

> [!TRAP] Calling record drawings "as-built verified by the architect."
